Frequently Asked Questions

We are a direct-pay platform — no insurance billing, no deductibles, no surprise costs. Flat-rate pricing means you know exactly what you pay before booking. Many patients find our rates comparable to or lower than their out-of-pocket insurance costs.

Same-day access is available for most visit types. Async reviews are typically completed within 2–4 hours. Live visits are usually available within hours of booking. Membership patients receive priority access ahead of standard queue.

Yes. Our providers can e-prescribe to your preferred pharmacy where clinically appropriate. Note: federal regulations prohibit controlled substance prescribing via remote care.

All visits, messages, and health information are handled on fully encrypted, HIPAA-compliant infrastructure. Your data is never sold or shared without explicit consent.

We currently serve Ohio, Virginia, West Virginia, Pennsylvania, and Michigan. Use the Find Care widget to verify your state before booking. We're expanding continuously.

When insurance is active, we verify your eligibility before your visit so you know any copay upfront. Claims are submitted to your insurer through Office Ally. Any remaining patient balance is handled through the Patient Ally portal. We will never bill you for more than your verified responsibility.

Office Ally is our EHR and practice management platform. Patient Ally is the patient-facing portal where you register, schedule appointments, complete intake forms, message your provider, and view your records. It is HIPAA-compliant and free for patients to use.

The Care Navigator is a symptom routing assistant — it helps match you to the right visit type based on your concern. It does not diagnose, treat, or provide medical advice. All clinical decisions are made exclusively by licensed healthcare professionals.

Submit Application
